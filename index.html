About Us

At WakimWorks LLC, our mission is to secure the digital foundations of human knowledge. We help museums, libraries, and educational institutions protect their digital collections with cloud-native security, resilience, and accessibility at the core. From automated S3 scanning to secure storage design, our focus is on building trusted systems that preserve history, research, and culture against loss or compromise. We believe the future of heritage preservation depends on secure infrastructure — and we’re here to make that future possible.

Founded by Jude Wakim, we aim to ensure that future generations can access authentic, untampered records from our past by fortifying the cloud systems and digital repositories that house them.
